The uncapped CPU figure for a still screen is an artefact of this bench and not of the
product: with no cap it asks for a frame as fast as it can and Desktop Duplication says
no in microseconds. The host's capture loop always sleeps to the frame budget.
gdi: a forced-full frame -- what every subscribe and every desktop switch costs -- was 321 KB in 106 ms.
dxgi: a forced-full frame -- what every subscribe and every desktop switch costs -- was 321 KB in 27 ms.

dxgi: idle sent something on 0 of 63 polls. A still screen should send nothing at all.
h264: idle sent something on 0 of 63 polls. A still screen should send nothing at all.

The 'video' condition is a moving field with noise, NOT a film: reproducible on purpose,
harsher than scrolling text and cheaper than real footage. Run --live against a real
video once before trusting it as an upper bound.
