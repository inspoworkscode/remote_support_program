# The Basic Version for Remote Workers

You are signing up for **the Basic version** of The Remote Support Program — the Basic Version for Remote Workers. Everything in these terms applies to it, with the changes in this section. **Where this section and the rest of these terms differ, this section applies.**

- **Your own computers.** The Basic version is for reaching computers that you, or the company you sign up for, **own**, or whose owner has **allowed you to reach them** — for example your computer at the office, or the computers in your own branch offices. You must not use it to support your customers or anybody else's computers; that needs the full version.
- **What it includes.** One person, who is the owner and the technician. Access to **{{BASIC_MACHINES}} computers**, and more if you pay for them — **{{BASIC_EXTRA}} a month for each one more, up to {{BASIC_MOST}} computers in all**. Each computer is reached either **unattended**, protected by an unattended password chosen when it is installed, or **attended**, where the person at the computer is asked to allow each connection. Remote control, file transfer, copy and paste, and printing. It does not include tickets, a branded app for customers, a way for the person at a computer to ask for help, or the other features of the full version.
- **The price.** After a free trial of 14 days the Basic version is **{{BASIC}} a month**, and {{BASIC_EXTRA}} a month for each computer you pay for past {{BASIC_MACHINES}}, paid by card in advance like the full version. More computers are added only when the owner sets the number on the Billing page and accepts the new monthly price shown first, and are charged from your next payment, with no part-month charges; paying for fewer brings the price down again from the payment after. Where section 2 speaks of the price of the package of seats, or of seats, it does not apply to the Basic version; the rest of section 2 does — the trial, paying monthly in advance, a payment more than 14 days late, and changes to our prices. Section 11 applies in full: you may cancel at any time, and we do not refund part of a month.
- **Upgrading and moving down.** If you upgrade to the full version, you will be shown its price and its terms, and accept them, before anything changes. You may move down to the Free version from the Billing page once no more than one computer is installed: your card subscription is cancelled at once, nothing more is charged, and the month already paid is not refunded.
- **Removing and replacing computers.** You may remove one of your computers to make room for another, up to two removals in a calendar month.
- **Changes to the Basic version.** We may change what the Basic version includes, or its limits, or stop offering it, by telling you by email at least 30 days before.
- **Section 3 still applies in full.** Even on your own computers, the people who use them must know that the computer can be reached remotely; a notice saying "Being controlled remotely" is shown on the screen during every session.
