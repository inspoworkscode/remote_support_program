# Your pilot agreement

You are signing up as one of our **pilot companies**, by the link we sent you. Everything in these terms applies to you, with the changes in this section. **Where this section and the rest of these terms differ, this section applies.**

- **Five seats free while you are a pilot company.** Your five seats cost nothing while you are a pilot company. There is no trial: your account is ready as soon as you sign up. A change to our prices under section 2 does not change this. Seats past five, if we allow them as section 2 says, are not free: we invoice them each month at the price section 2 gives.
- **What we ask of you.** A pilot is there for you to use the service with your own customers and tell us what works and what does not.
- **Ending the pilot.** We may end your pilot by telling you by email at least 30 days before. The email says which of these happens on that day: your company moves to the price in section 2, with 14 days to add a card before the service pauses; or the agreement ends, as section 11 describes. You may end the agreement at any time, as section 11 says.
- **The rest of section 2.** Where section 2 speaks of the trial or of the price of the package, it does not apply to you while you are a pilot company. Everything else in it does.
- **The pilot price is for your company only** and cannot be passed to another company.
